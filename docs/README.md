## Making your own demos

1. Read the [scene design lessons](guides/scene_design_lessons.md).
2. Browse [`demos/`](demos/) for a scene close to your idea; each page links to its shader.
3. Follow [making your own scene](demos/README.md#making-your-own-scene).
