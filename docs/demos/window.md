## Brief

From the corner illusions brief, part 1, section 2.2 ("Window into a bigger room, 20 s").

**Sees**: a rectangular portal opens on each wall and the ceiling. Behind them is a larger, deeper space (a gridded room several meters deep, or a starfield), with consistent depth across all three portals.
**Build**: portals from 1.1. The `behind` scene is one 3D environment rendered from the viewer camera, so all three windows look into the same space. Animate portals growing from the seams outward.

## As built

Portals open on each surface onto one deep gridded room rendered from the viewer's eye, so the depth agrees across all three. Three tempos: one slow portal in cool colours, snaps on the beat in new colours, then three nested windows. Each window floods with colour, flares, then fades to its border lines.
