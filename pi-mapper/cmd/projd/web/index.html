Corner calibration

Tell the Pi where the corner is. Everything else is worked out from these four dots.

  1. Press Start. The projector shows four dots and their lines.
  2. Put the white dot on the point where both walls meet the ceiling.
  3. Slide red along the left wall/ceiling edge, blue along the right one, and yellow down the wall/wall edge, until each line covers its edge.
  4. Press Save. Dots far from the corner give the best result.

Your pointer shows on the projection as a green crosshair while it's over this view.

Adjust the lens only if scenes look stretched after saving.

Warp calibration

Corner-pin flat video onto a surface. Not needed for the live corner scenes, which use Corner calibration.

Drag a corner, or drag inside to move the whole picture. With the alignment grid on, the corners show on the wall as 1 red, 2 green, 3 blue, 4 yellow.
